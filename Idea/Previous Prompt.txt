Yes, I think this is actually a very good hackathon idea, especially if your project already has a BPM platform with multiple case types such as account opening, account transfer, address change, additional document requests, etc.

What makes it valuable is that you're not just creating another dashboard. You're solving a genuine operational pain point:

People spend a lot of time opening multiple tabs, reading comments, checking workflow history, navigating documents, and understanding the current state of a case.

A "Case 360 Summary" capability can significantly reduce investigation and servicing time.

Why I think it is strong
A user enters a Case ID and immediately gets:

Case creation date
Current status
Case summary generated by GenAI
Workflow stage and pending action
Comments summary
Complete audit/history timeline
Document inventory
Customer details
SLA status
Next recommended action
